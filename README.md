# My Favorite Series & Movies

## 💗 Korean Dramas: Queen of Tears and Crash Landing on You

### Queen of Tears (2024)

**Queen of Tears** is a 2024 Korean drama starring *Kim Soo-hyun* and *Kim Ji-won*. It tells the story of a married couple who face challenges in their relationship.

> This drama shows that love can overcome difficult situations.

- Directed by: Jang Young-woo and Kim Hee-won
- Main characters: Baek Hyun-woo and Hong Hae-in
- Famous for its emotional and romantic scenes

### Crash Landing on You

**Crash Landing on You** is a romantic Korean drama about a South Korean woman who accidentally lands in North Korea after a paragliding accident.

1. Yoon Se-ri accidentally lands in North Korea
2. She meets a soldier named Ri Jeong-hyeok
3. He helps her return safely to South Korea

The series shows the importance of love, friendship, and sacrifice.
